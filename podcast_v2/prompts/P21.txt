Create a podcast script for the "AI Convo Cast" podcast, a daily AI news and technology podcast. This is a special episode that covers ONE topic in depth, given below. We will be adding a standard intro and outro on our end, so only generate the main body of the episode script and always start with:
"Today we will be..." and then say in one sentence what this episode on the topic will cover.
Since we will add intro and outro on our end, do not make references to the podcast. Do not summarize the episode at the end; it is fine to end after the final segment. Avoid phrases like "this is particularly interesting," "Reflecting on this," or "Imagine a world where," and avoid broad statements about the AI industry as a whole.
Structure the episode as 3 to 5 segments on the topic, following the research brief's recommended running order unless there is a clear reason not to. Cover every angle named in the topic (for example release, early adoption, feedback, future trends), giving each real time. Open with the most compelling angle that listeners have not already heard; anything the brief marks as previously covered gets one or two sentences of recap and must not open the episode. When covering future trends, attribute predictions to the people making them and present them as views, not facts. Where the evidence supports it, also cover what is new and how it works, adoption and community feedback, perspectives from the people involved and respected, well-known voices in AI, and limitations or what to watch next. Use smooth spoken transitions between segments with no headings. Stay on the topic; do not add unrelated news.
The script should be written in plain, conversational language that is casual, informed, and assumes the listener is familiar with AI models generally and just wants to hear the facts. Explain technical concepts simply. Make it interesting with concrete details, contradictions, tradeoffs and stakes from the source material. Use one or two attributed quotes per segment from the people involved (founders, lab leaders, researchers, company representatives) or respected voices in AI and developer communities, naming who said it and their role, then briefly explain what the quote shows. Never invent or alter a quote; only use quotes present in the source material or found through search. Only read off metrics, figures, percentages, benchmark scores or pricing when they support a clear point, comparison, tradeoff or user impact.
If web search is enabled for this step, use it only when needed to verify a key detail, fill an important gap, or find a quote from the people involved when a segment has none; one or two searches at most.
Design the content for a natural 6 to 9 minute main-body runtime. The script should generally be about 5,000 to 7,500 characters, and may run up to about 8,500 characters when the material is genuinely strong and the extra time adds quotes, perspective or useful detail rather than filler.
Output must be pure plain text (UTF-8 encoded) with no hyperlinks, citations, markdown formatting, extraneous symbols, or any headings like "Summary." Do not include any non-text elements or words/phrases ending in ".com." Avoid numbering or bullet points and refrain from using corny sayings. Please also always remove dashes from names (e.g. GPT-4.5 should be GPT 4.5). When converting this text to speech, automatically replace 'GPT 4o' with 'GPT Four-Oh', 'DALL-E' with 'Dolly'. Avoid corny transitions like "speaking of". Do not use the phrase "what makes this particularly interesting". If discussing evals, explain why they are significant compared to competitors and do not just list them off.
