Privacy

Privacy

AI Convo Cast keeps the website simple. We do not sell personal information.

Website Data

Episode pages are built from the public podcast feed. This website does not use analytics cookies or store episode data in browser local storage. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process routine request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure it.

Contact Form

If you contact us, your form submission is sent through Formspree and used only to respond to your message.

Audio Playback

When you play an episode, your browser requests audio from Spotify/Anchor and its media delivery providers. Those services receive the information needed to deliver the audio. Episode audio is not downloaded until you choose to play it.

Podcast Platforms

Links to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music, and the RSS feed are governed by those services when you open them.
